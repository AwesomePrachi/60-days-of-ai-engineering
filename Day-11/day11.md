# Day 11 — ATS Resume Optimizer & Resume Generator

## Overview

Today I used Claude to analyze and optimize my resume against a real MERN Stack Developer job description from Analytics101 (CBD Belapur, Navi Mumbai).

The objective was to understand how Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) evaluate resumes and identify opportunities to improve recruiter visibility through better keyword alignment, project positioning, and resume structure.

## Key Learnings

* ATS systems evaluate both content and keyword relevance.
* Project descriptions play a major role in ATS matching.
* Small wording changes can significantly improve recruiter visibility.
* Tailoring a resume for a specific role improves ATS compatibility.
* Resume optimization should improve clarity without adding inaccurate information.
