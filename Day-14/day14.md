# Day 14 – AI Job Red Flag Detector

## Overview

Today, I learned how to use Claude to analyze a job opportunity before applying for it.

Usually, when we see a job posting, we focus on the role, company name, or tech stack. But there can be hidden signals related to work culture, salary transparency, workload, and overall company environment.

Using Claude's AI Job Red Flag Detector, I analyzed a MERN Stack Developer job posting and generated a detailed risk assessment report.

## What I Did

* Collected a MERN Stack Developer Job Description
* Gathered company information and employee review insights
* Used the AI Job Red Flag Detector prompt in Claude
* Generated a risk analysis report
* Reviewed red flags, positive signals, and the final recommendation

## Results

### Overall Risk Score

**58/100 (Moderate–High Risk)**

### Red Flags Identified

* Salary information was not disclosed
* Work-life balance concerns appeared in employee reviews
* Compensation-related complaints were mentioned by some employees
* Employee experiences were mixed across different reviews
* Some responsibilities and expectations were not clearly defined

### Positive Signals

* Suitable technical requirements for freshers
* MERN Stack skills aligned with the role
* Opportunities to learn and grow
* Established company presence
* Potential career growth opportunities

## Final Verdict

⚠️ **Apply with Caution**

The role looked good from a technical perspective, but there were a few concerns that should be clarified during the interview process before making a decision.

## Key Learnings

* A job application is a two-way evaluation process.
* Candidates should evaluate companies just as companies evaluate candidates.
* AI can help identify risks that are easy to miss in a Job Description.
* Employee reviews can provide valuable insights about company culture.
* Asking the right interview questions can help validate potential concerns.

## Conclusion

This exercise showed me how AI can be used beyond coding and content generation. It can also act as a career research assistant, helping job seekers evaluate opportunities and make more informed career decisions.
