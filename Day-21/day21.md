# Day 21 — Privacy Intelligence with Claude AI

## Objective

Build an interactive Digital Privacy Intelligence Dashboard that visualizes a sample user's digital footprint, privacy exposure, estimated tracking risks, and actionable privacy recommendations.

---

## What I Built

A premium single-page HTML dashboard that transforms a sample digital footprint into an interactive privacy intelligence report.

The dashboard includes:

* Digital Footprint Score
* Privacy Score
* Exposure Heatmap
* Company Exposure Ranking
* Data Collection Matrix
* Ecosystem Concentration Analysis
* Risk Radar
* Digital Twin Profile (Facts vs Estimates)
* Most Valuable Data Assets
* Privacy Improvement Simulator
* Security Recommendations
* Final Verdict

---

## Tech Stack

* HTML5
* CSS3
* JavaScript (Vanilla)
* Claude AI

---

## Key Learnings

* Learned how digital services contribute to an overall digital footprint.
* Understood the difference between factual information and AI-generated estimates.
* Explored how ecosystem concentration can increase privacy exposure.
* Practiced designing a modern cybersecurity-style dashboard with interactive visualizations.

---

## Key Insight

Using multiple services from the same company can significantly increase digital footprint concentration and overall privacy exposure.
