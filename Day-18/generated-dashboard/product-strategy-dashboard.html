📋 Summary
▼
The team reviewed progress on the AI Resume Analyzer project. Key discussion areas included a drop in user onboarding completion rates over the last two weeks, slower-than-expected API performance during peak traffic, and a disagreement around the timing of the planned July 15 marketing launch. A proposal was made to simplify onboarding from 7 screens to 3 screens; no final decision was reached. One formal decision was made: usability testing must be conducted before approving final onboarding changes.
